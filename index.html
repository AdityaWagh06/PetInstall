Questions

Good to know

Do I need a CSS file too?

No. Everything the pets need, their drawings, styles and animations, is inside the one petbuddy.js file. Your own CSS can't break them and theirs can't touch your page.

Does it slow my site down?

No. It is one small file with no libraries. Each pet runs a single animation loop, and pets that sit in place rest when they are scrolled out of view.

Will it clash with my styles?

No. Each pet lives inside its own Shadow DOM, so your CSS can't break it and its CSS can't touch your page.

Does it work with React, Vue, WordPress, Wix or Shopify?

Yes. It is a standard web component, so anything that can render HTML can show a pet. The playground has ready snippets.

Can I have more than one pet?

Yes. Add as many <pet-buddy> tags as you like, like Bubu and Dudu on this page. Want them to play with each other? Use <pet-duo pets="bubu dudu"> and they hug, high five, share snacks and more.

What about people who prefer less motion?

Pets respect the reduced motion setting. They still change faces, but they don't jump, walk or spin.
